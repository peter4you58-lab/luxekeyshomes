DROP PROPERTY PHOTOS HERE TO REPLACE THE PLACEHOLDERS
-----------------------------------------------------
The 8 sample listings look for photos named prop-1.jpg ... prop-8.jpg in this
folder. Until a file exists, a branded placeholder (icon + city label) shows.

To use real photos:
  1. Get photos you're allowed to use — your own inspection photos are ideal
     (and match the "authentic photography" pitch). Free stock also works:
     Unsplash (https://unsplash.com) or Pexels (https://pexels.com) are free
     for commercial use.
  2. Rename them prop-1.jpg through prop-8.jpg and drop them in this folder.
  3. Refresh — the real photos replace the placeholders automatically.

Landlord listings created in-app already show their uploaded photos — this file
convention is only for the 8 built-in sample listings.

Matching order (so photos suit the listing):

  prop-4: mini flat, Asaba, Delta        prop-8: 2-bed flat (agent), Surulere
